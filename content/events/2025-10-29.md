### “💻 AI-Powered performance testing: exploring the possibilities”

[Leandro Melendez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/leandromelendez)

As [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) continues to transform industries, performance testing is on the cusp of exciting changes. This session explores potential AI applications in performance testing, focusing on the possibilities. Things like AI correlations creating bulletproof scripts, assisting load test scenario creation, and many others.
Whether you're a performance tester, QA professional, or just curious about the next wave of innovation, this talk will challenge you to think beyond current limitations and envision what AI could bring to our field in the near future.💘

#### Bio

[Leandro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/leandromelendez) is helping everyone to ramp up on their observability, QA and performance practices.
He has over 20 years of experience in IT and over 15 in the performance testing practice where he served multiple S&P500 customers all over the USA, Mexico, Canada, Brazil, India, Austria, etc.
Author of the performance testing blog Señor Performo (www.srperf.com) where he curates a diverse set of learning material for performance testers and engineers together with a couple of YouTube channels (https://youtube.com/@srPerfEsp and https://youtube.com/@srPerfEng).
He is an international public speaker participating in multiple conferences, events and webinars, with keynotes, workshops and multiple talks on his belt.
And last, author of "The Hitchhikers Guide To Load Testing Projects", a fun walkthrough that will guide you through the phases or levels of an IT load testing project. https://amzn.to/37wqpyx
