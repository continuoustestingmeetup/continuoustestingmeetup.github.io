### “Achieving continuous quality through people, process, tools & outcomes”

[Stuart Day](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stuart-day)

[Shifting left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift-left_testing) is no longer enough.
In fact, it never has been as it often focused solely on testing and not all the possible quality enablers. This is where the concept of continuous quality comes in, but what is it really, and how can it be achieved? In his talk, Stuart Day will describe his continuous quality journey (which started back in 2020), what it really is and how to start on your own journey to achieving it.

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

1. be able to describe what Quality is
2. understand what a Culture of Quality is and how to build one
3. understand what Continuous Quality is and what the quality enablers are
4. learn about the 6 Qs of quality and how to apply them to support continuous quality
5. how to measure progress

#### Bio

With 25+ years in tech, quality engineering and transformation, [Stuart Day](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stuart-day) is a senior leader passionate about people, trust and psychological safety. As an [ICF](https://coachingfederation.org/) Team Coach, [Agile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agile_software_development) Coach and passionate Quality Leader, he enables teams to grow and lead impactful, customer-focused change. He also gives back as a podcast host, speaker and mentor, sharing insights to inspire and grow our future leaders.
