### “Multi-agentic architectures for test automation”

[Robin Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/polymorphicrobin/)

The future of software testing isn’t just about faster tools—it’s about smarter collaboration between humans and [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) agents. This session explores how to build open-source, multi-agentic systems that transform testing by leveraging specialized agents for development, design, and verification. We’ll dive into key patterns like [Reflection](http://software-pattern.org/Reflection), Tool Use, Planning, and Multi-Agent collaboration, showing how agents can think, plan, and work together. You’ll also learn how agents like Hercules can accelerate the entire software development lifecycle by working alongside human teams.

#### Bio

[Robin Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/polymorphicrobin/) is the human at the helm for [TestZeus](https://testzeus.com/), the company behind world's first open source testing agent. He is a versatile engineering leader with more than 15 years of experience in software delivery across startups, scale-ups and enterprises. He is a System 1 thinker, and has led products contributing to $10M ARR. With a metrics-driven approach, he has elevated engineering maturity of product teams for diverse domains such as BFSI, EdTech, Retail, and Developer Experience. Beyond work, he mentors at [ADPList](https://adplist.org/) and [Plato](https://platocommunity.substack.com/about), contributes to open-source projects like [Selenium](https://www.selenium.dev/) and has authored books and international courses on software testing. He is also a recognized speaker at international events such as [Dreamforce](https://www.salesforce.com/dreamforce/) (by [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/eu/)) and Selenium Conference.
