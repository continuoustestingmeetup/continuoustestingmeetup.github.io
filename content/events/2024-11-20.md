### “Revolutionizing Salesforce Testing: AI-Powered Automation with Copado Robotic Testing”

[Asha Rani](https://www.linkedin.com/in/asha-rani-bb1049b9/)

In this talk, we'll explore how [Copado Robotic Testing](https://www.copado.com/product-overview/copado-robotic-testing) (CRT) is transforming Salesforce testing practices. We'll dive into the unique challenges of Salesforce testing and how [AI-powered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) automation addresses these issues. Key topics include:

- overview of CRT's features: QWords, Flow Editor, AI-driven Salesforce Guided Authoring, and self-healing tests
- live demonstration of creating and running a test case
- real-world benefits and impact on release cycles
- best practices for integrating CRT into your workflow
- future trends in AI-powered Salesforce testing

Attendees will gain insights into leveraging CRT to significantly reduce testing time, improve coverage, and accelerate their Salesforce development process.

#### Bio

[Asha Rani](https://www.linkedin.com/in/asha-rani-bb1049b9/) is a Senior Test Architect at WilcoSource, specializing in Copado Robotic Testing. Recognized as a Copado Champion and featured in the Women in DevOps spotlight, Asha Rani has 17 years of experience in software testing with a focus on revolutionizing Salesforce testing practices. At WilcoSource, she leads the implementation and optimization of Copado Robotic Testing solutions, helping organizations transform their testing processes for improved efficiency and reliability.
