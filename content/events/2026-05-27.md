### "Lead the testing"

[Jesper Ottosen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jespero2/)

If you wonder how to get into [test leadership](https://www.istqb.org/certifications/test-manager) then this talk is for you!
It's about understanding test leadership, about all the [kinds of testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_testing)
and the activities that go with leading testing. To lead testing does not
require a formal management role - but it does require initiative and outlook
besides the daily tickets and [ceremonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrum_(software_development)#Events). Also it's about leading the testing
that is happening, not about leading the testers. The testing these days has
many sources - and there are many types of testing that need people in the lead.
Join me in this conversation on growing into a leadership testing role.

[Jesper Ottosen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jespero2/) primarily leads testing activities in programs and projects that address
