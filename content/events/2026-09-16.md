### "You Didn't Learn It, You Googled It Faster: Learning with AI"

[Oleksandr Romanov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/oleksandr-romanov/)

The amount of information we need to process every day is enormous. The job expectations are high and grow constantly. We need to [learn new skills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning), and it seems that with [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) we can learn anything almost instantly.

But is it true? Are we learning a new skill or just ... becoming familiar with some terms? How can we [ingrain new knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-term_memory) so we can use it without any assistance? Is it possible to do it, and is it ... worth doing it today?

In this session, I will tell you what learning is and how AI can be a good or bad tool while you are achieving your learning goals.
